My Buyers — Creating the Sense of Missing Out

How to signal opportunity and risk on the table row itself — without the financier needing to open every drawer.

The real scenario: Financier opens My Buyers at 9am. Scans the list in under 10 seconds. If nothing jumps out, they move on.
The signal logic used across all variants:  🟠 Opportunity = Platform MTD growing but your share is below 15% — you're leaving business on the table.   🔴 Slipping = Your disc. MTD dropped MoM — you're doing less than last month.   🟡 Expiry = Limit expires within 60 days — action needed.   🟢 Healthy = No signal needed.
