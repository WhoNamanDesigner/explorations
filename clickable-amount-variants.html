Fin. MTD & YTD — Clickable Amount + Pill Variants
5 approaches to making the amount a hyperlink while keeping the contextual pill unambiguous.
Variant 1 Pill below · Appearance only
Amount is blue + underlines on hover. Pill stays below but gets a rounded grey tag shape with cursor:default — no pointer, no hover state. Same placement, clearer affordance split.
